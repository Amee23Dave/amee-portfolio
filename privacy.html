Privacy

Privacy

Last updated — August 2026

This website is a portfolio. It is built with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, and does not run a database, a server-side application or an account system of any kind. There is no reason for it to collect personal information beyond what is needed to reply to a message sent directly.

What this site collects

Browsing this site does not require submitting any personal information. There is no contact form, no login and no tracking cookie set by this website itself. If you choose to email or message Amee Dave through one of the links on this site, only the information you decide to share is received.

External video & media

Project videos may be delivered through an external video hosting provider — for example Vimeo, Cloudflare Stream or Bunny Stream. These providers may process standard technical data, such as IP address, device type and approximate location, in order to deliver video content reliably. That processing is governed by the relevant provider's own privacy policy, not by this website.

External links

This site links out to Instagram and LinkedIn. Once you leave this site through one of those links, your activity is governed by that platform's own privacy policy and terms, not by this website.

Analytics

This site may use privacy-friendly, cookie-free analytics — such as Plausible — to understand overall traffic, for example how many people visit a page, without tracking individuals or storing personal data across other sites. Google Analytics and advertising trackers are not used.

Contact

If you have a question about this privacy note, reach out at work.ameedave@email.com.
